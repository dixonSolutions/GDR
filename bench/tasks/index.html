GDR computer-use benchmark

Five desktop tasks, each scored by the page rather than by the agent.

Aim

1 · Turn every shape red

Precise clicking on targets from 26 to 80 pixels, mixed with decoys.
State

2 · Cycle every swatch to green

Count steps through a colour cycle without overshooting.
Reading

3 · Read the article, then answer

Small body text, five questions, answers only in the article.
Navigation

4 · Collect the slide codes

Keyboard paging plus small monospace codes to transcribe.
Scroll & write

5 · Summarise the front page

Scroll a long page, gather four facts, compose and submit.
